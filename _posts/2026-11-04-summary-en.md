### [Background Plane Noise Reveals Video Location](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/a-sound-that-leaks-your-exact-location/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A novel OSINT technique demonstrates how background plane noise in videos can be used to determine exact location. By analyzing the Doppler curves of these sounds and cross-referencing them with ADS-B flight data, researchers can pinpoint where a video was recorded. This method works with almost imperceptible plane sounds found in many video soundtracks and correlates their speed, distance, and timing gaps to triangulate the precise filming location.

rss · Simon Willison · Nov 3, 05:32

**「Background Concepts」** ADS-B \(Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast\) is an airspace surveillance system where aircraft broadcast their position, speed, and other flight parameters. The Doppler effect describes how sound waves change frequency based on the relative motion between the source and listener, which creates distinctive curves in audio spectrograms when aircraft pass by. This novel OSINT technique leverages these two concepts to extract location data from otherwise inaudible background aircraft noise in videos.

**「Privacy Implications」** This OSINT technique creates a new privacy vulnerability for video content creators and consumers, as background plane noise can now be used to determine exact filming locations. Content creators who wish to maintain location privacy should consider removing or masking audio tracks containing background air traffic to prevent unintended geolocation.
