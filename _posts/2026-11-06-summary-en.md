### [Databases as Global Variables](https://seangoedecke.com/a-database-is-a-store-for-global-variables/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

rss · Sean Goedecke · Nov 6, 00:00

**「Background」** The article explores why databases are acceptable despite functioning essentially as global variables, which contradicts common programming advice against using global variables. This creates an apparent paradox in software design that deserves examination.

**「Solution」** The author examines several potential explanations for why databases work despite being global variables. While ACID properties and type safety offer some benefits, the most convincing reasons are that databases are more explicit \(requiring connection objects and query functions rather than direct variable access\) and that we&\#x27;ve established norms around their limited use cases. These factors mitigate the implicit coupling and data passing problems that make regular global variables problematic. The author notes that these benefits are contingent on usage patterns rather than inherent properties of databases themselves.

**「Takeaway」** The article&\#x27;s core insight is that common software engineering advice contains inconsistencies, highlighting that much of what we consider &\#x27;best practices&\#x27; are actually just loose heuristics rather than absolute rules.
